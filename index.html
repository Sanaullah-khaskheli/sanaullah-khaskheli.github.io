My Journey

I started out in Electrical Engineering (Electronics) at Sukkur IBA University, with hands-on experience in embedded systems and robotics hardware. Teaching Robotics & AI as an instructor, and later working as a Research Associate on an AI-based flood alert chatbot, pulled me toward machine learning and applied AI research — and eventually into an MS in Computer Science focused on vision-language models and autonomous navigation.

Research

Semantic Topological Mapping

MS thesis developing a training-free, content-adaptive keyframe selection method and a loop-closure pipeline fusing DINOv2/DINOv3 structural descriptors with CLIP/SigLIP semantic embeddings, evaluated on KITTI, KITTI-360, and AberCampus00.

Vision-Language Models

Benchmarking self-supervised vision transformers and vision-language embedding models for place recognition and scene understanding under real-world appearance change.

Human-Robot Interaction

Programming NAO and Pepper humanoid robots at the CRAIB Lab for interactive demonstrations, including a ball-kicking-to-goal behaviour combining object detection, locomotion control, and goal alignment.

Edge AI & Cybersecurity

Edge-deployed CNNs for real-time plant disease detection, and multi-task learning frameworks for cybersecurity applications, alongside teaching applied cybersecurity.

Beyond Research

Preikestolen, Norway — during a cabin trip while on research exchange at NTNU.

Currently

Lab Instructor teaching Cybersecurity at Sukkur IBA University, alongside ongoing MS research on semantic topological mapping. Open to research collaborations, academic discussions, and PhD opportunities.
