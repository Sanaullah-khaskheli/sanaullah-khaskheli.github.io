My Journey

I started out in Electrical Engineering at Sukkur IBA University, with a foundation in embedded systems and automation. Teaching Robotics & AI as an instructor, and later working as a Research Associate on an AI-based flood alert chatbot, pulled me toward machine learning, NLP, and applied AI research — and eventually into an MS in Computer Science focused on robotics and computer vision.

Research

Visual SLAM & Topological Mapping

MS thesis on entropy-gated fusion of visual and semantic descriptors for robust topological mapping in mobile robotics, comparing DINOv2, CLIP, and SigLIP across loop-closure and node-selection pipelines.

Foundation Model Applications

Benchmarking vision-language and self-supervised foundation models for place recognition and scene understanding under real-world appearance change.

IoT & Industrial Security

Evidential deep learning architectures for zero-day intrusion detection in Industrial IoT, built on Edge-IIoTset with a leave-one-attack-out protocol.

Applied Deep Learning

Deep learning applications spanning medical imaging and agricultural monitoring (edge-AI plant health monitoring at NTNU), with an emphasis on deployable pipelines.

Currently

Visiting Faculty at the Department of Computer System Engineering, Sukkur IBA University — teaching Digital Signal Processing, Machine Learning, and Cybersecurity — alongside ongoing MS research on topological mapping. Open to research collaborations, academic discussions, and PhD opportunities.
